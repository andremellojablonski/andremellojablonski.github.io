About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at EPGE–FGV, interested in Macroeconomics, Labor Economics, and Environmental Economics.

My research is funded by the Instituto de Pesquisa Econômica Aplicada (IPEA), where I am a research assistant.

Previously, I've earned a master’s degree in Analysis and Policy in Economics (APE) from the Paris School of Economics and a bachelor’s degree in Economics from the University of São Paulo.
